Donghyeok Park

Reliable Physical AI for Aerospace Manufacturing

I am a robotics engineer at Korea Aerospace Industries (KAI), where I develop and deploy robotic systems for real-world aerospace manufacturing environments, including humanoid and collaborative robots.

My research interests center on reliable autonomous robotics: integrating perception, learning, planning, and control so that robots can operate robustly under uncertainty in complex physical environments.

Through industrial projects, I have worked on vision-guided manipulation, SLAM-based navigation, robot-cell integration, and closed-loop process validation. I aim to advance robotic systems that are safe, adaptive, and generalizable beyond controlled laboratory settings.

Email · Google Scholar · LinkedIn · CV

Research Interests

I am interested in building autonomous robotic systems that can reliably perceive, reason, and act in real-world environments.

  • Perception and 3D scene understanding for robotic manipulation
  • Reliable localization, navigation, and motion planning under uncertainty
  • Learning-based methods for robust and adaptive robot behavior
  • Vision-guided manipulation and closed-loop task execution
  • Sim-to-real transfer and deployment of embodied AI systems
  • Physical AI for manufacturing, logistics, and human-centered environments

My long-term goal is to bridge research and deployment by developing scalable robotic systems that can be trusted in safety- and quality-critical settings.

Selected Industry Projects

Government-Funded Research Project · 2025–2026

Humanoid Logistics Automation for Aerospace Manufacturing

Developed a Physical AI framework for humanoid-assisted logistics in an aerospace manufacturing environment. The system combined 2D LiDAR-based SLAM, YOLOv8-based part verification, and perception-driven task execution.

Key Contributions

  • Designed SLAM-based navigation strategies for inter-facility logistics
  • Integrated vision-based verification before manipulation tasks
  • Developed perception-driven autonomous task-execution logic
  • Implemented teleoperation-based data collection for picking and loading

Technologies: Python, YOLOv8, 2D LiDAR SLAM, RGB Vision, RB-Y1

Government-Funded Research Project · 2025–2026

Collaborative-Robot Drilling Automation for Aerospace Assembly

Developed an automated drilling system using AGV-mounted collaborative robots, machine vision, PLC-controlled drilling, and closed-loop pose correction for aerospace assembly operations.

Key Contributions

  • Engineered a multi-robot drilling workflow for complex assemblies
  • Implemented vision-based hole detection and verification
  • Developed pose correction before each drilling operation
  • Integrated robot motion with PLC signals for synchronized execution
  • Built a closed-loop validation pipeline for process consistency

Technologies: Python, Machine Vision, PLC Integration, Collaborative Robots, AGV Systems
